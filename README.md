# Crop Disease and Pest Identification System

A mobile application designed to help farmers identify visible diseases, pests, and abnormal conditions affecting their crops through a short video.

## Overview

Farmers often need to inspect several plants before determining what problem is affecting their crops. Taking and submitting individual photos for every plant can be inconvenient, especially when multiple plants are affected.

This project provides a simpler approach: **the farmer records a short video of the crops, and the application analyzes the plants shown in the video.**

The system identifies the crops present in the recording and examines their visible conditions. Detected diseases, pests, and flower-related abnormalities are associated with the corresponding crop or plant. The results are then presented to the farmer together with information and recommendations that can help them understand how to respond to the identified problem.

## How It Works

The general process is:

1. **Record a Video**

   * The farmer records a short video showing one or more crops.
   * Multiple plants or different crops may be included in the same recording.

2. **Submit the Video**

   * The video is uploaded through the application for analysis.

3. **Analyze the Crops**

   * The system examines the plants shown in the video.
   * It identifies the crop and looks for visible signs of diseases, pests, and abnormal flower conditions.

4. **Match the Findings**

   * Identified problems are associated with the corresponding crop or plant.
   * This allows the system to distinguish which condition belongs to which crop when multiple crops are present.

5. **Provide the Results**

   * The application summarizes the detected conditions for the plants found in the video.
   * The farmer can view the identified problem and its corresponding information.

6. **Provide Recommendations**

   * Based on the identified condition, the application provides recommendations intended to help the farmer understand appropriate actions and possible ways to manage the problem.

## Purpose

The project aims to make crop inspection more convenient by allowing farmers to examine **multiple plants through a single video instead of submitting individual images one at a time**.

It is intended to serve as an accessible source of crop health information and guidance, particularly for farmers who may have limited access to agricultural specialists.

## Project Scope

The system focuses on:

* Identification of supported crops
* Identification of visible crop diseases
* Identification of visible pest damage
* Assessment of flower conditions where applicable
* Analysis of multiple plants within a single video
* Providing information and recommendations based on detected conditions

The system is intended to assist farmers with **initial identification and guidance**. Its results should not be considered a replacement for professional agricultural diagnosis.
